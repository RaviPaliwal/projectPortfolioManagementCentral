1 Introduction 
1.1 Company Background 
Iarnród Éireann (IÉ) is the national railway operator in the Republic of Ireland which operates a 
mix of Intercity and Commuter services, servicing 146 stations and employing circa 5,000 staff 
nationwide. 
Intercity services mainly operate from Dublin and link the main cities of Ireland including Cork, 
Limerick, Tralee, Waterford, Galway, Westport, Sligo, Belfast and Rosslare Europort. An 
extensive Commuter service operates within the Dublin region and surro unding counties, while 
Cork and Limerick also have commuter services on a smaller scale.  
IÉ is also responsible for the maintenance and development of the rail infrastructure including 
the track, overhead lines, stations and the rail fleet required to operate the service. IÉ also 
operates Port services at Rosslare Europort.  
With safety as our number one corporate goal, IÉ’s corporate identify and corporate objectives is 
critically dependant on its people, with their skills, training, professionalism, adaptability, 
organisation and efficiency all considered central to our success.  
IÉ is largely separated into Railway Undertaking (IERU) which manage the operations of the rolling 
stock, Infrastructure Management ( IEIM) which operate and maintain and Capital Investments 
(CI) which design and build railway assets and associated infrastructure (Buildings and Stations).  
CI is the division within IÉ which plans and delivers infrastructure and fleet investment on the IÉ 
rail network. CI undertakes the construction of large-scale multi-year infrastructure projects and 
can vary from small bridge and rail upgrades, up to multi-year programmes. These projects entail 
the design and tendering early phases of construction through to the building and commissioning 
aspects associated with safety critical infrastructure projects. 
1.2 Invitation to Tender (ITT) 
IÉ CI invites qualified and experienced service providers to submit tenders for the provision of a 
Project, Programme, and Portfolio Management (PPM) Solution built utilising IÉs Microsoft Power 
Platform. The objective of the PPM solution is the following: 
 
• Provide a centralised platform for project, programme, and portfolio oversight, providing 
a single source of the truth for portfolio performance. 
• Standardise governance, assurance, and reporting across teams. 
• Standardise data collection by introducing consistent data -entry forms, workflows, and 
structures across project and portfolio reporting. 
• Improve visibility of delivery performance, risks, financials etc.  
• Support data-driven decision making through dashboards and other reporting metrics. 
• Enable scalable functionality aligned with future organisational needs. 
 
The successful supplier will provide and configure predeveloped Power Platform templates 
and/or components to rapidly establish a digital PPM data capture and reporting solution. The 
core requirement is the quick delivery of a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) with the ability to scale 
the solution. The PPM MVP solution must provide an option to expand licensing, functionality, 
and integrations in future deployments.

2 Background and Solution Requirements 
2.1 About Iarnród Éireann Capital Investments 
IÉ CI manages a large portfolio of projects ranging in size, scale and complexity. CI have 
experienced a significant increase in the volume of large capital projects, programmes and 
increased value of contracts. CI are taking appropriate steps to transition fro m a static, 
fragmented and manual ways of working to an integrated, centralised, scalable and intelligent 
best-practice digital environment. 
CI’s vision is to develop a best -in-class Project Management Office (PMO) Project Controls & 
Reporting function, ensuring accurate reporting and integrated project, programme and portfolio 
management. To create a digitally enabled ecosystem that delivers c onsistent project controls, 
improved process and system integration, real -time metrics, trends and insights, improved 
information management and robust governance and assurance . The objective of these 
initiatives is to  provide the  CI leadership with a single source of truth and confidence in the 
reporting and data being generated. The transition to a digital ecosystem will enable automated 
reporting, streamlined workflows, improved performance tracking, dynamic risk integration and  
schedule optimisation.  
2.2 Users 
CI have a requirement to categorise users of the solution into three broad classifications, System 
Administrator, Standard User and Team Member User.

and overall PPM platform configuration.

permissions to view and interact with core PPM capabilities.

2.3 Scope of Work 
This Scope of Works defines the phased delivery of a PPM solution built on Microsoft Power 
Platform using pre‑developed templates and/or components. The delivery model is designed to 
provide flexibility, scalability, and full cost transparency. It shall allow CI to evaluate the solution 
before committing to full deployment and long‑term support. 
The project will be delivered in three phases: 
• MVP Pilot Deployment 
• Solution Enhancements (Optional) 
• Full Deployment + Post Deployment Support (Optional) 
System Enhancements, Full Deployment and Post Deployment Support elements of this tender 
will only be triggered upon successful completion and evaluation of the MVP Pliot. 
The objectives of this approach are as follows: 
• Deploy a scalable and user ‑friendly PPM system built on IÉs own Microsoft Power 
Platform. 
• Allow CI to validate suitability through a controlled MVP Pilot. 
• Provide flexibility for CI to adopt the solution in its ‘vanilla’ configuration or proceed with 
enhancements based on pilot findings. 
• Establish an optional long‑term support framework. 
MVP Pilot Deployment 
The provision of an MVP using pre‑built Power Platform PPM templates and/or components with 
minimal “vanilla” configuration. The objective is to validate core functionality and assess 
whether further development is required. 
The MVP will be deployed to a pilot group of up to 20 users, supporting up to 10 live projects over 
approx. 3 reporting cycles (4 weekly periods).
